## How to paste a post

| Admin form field | What to paste | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article tab | Use it for every post here | The Update (ticker) tab is for one-line headlines of up to 140 characters; none are needed for this archive |
| Title | The heading of each post, unchanged | Titles are the papers' own titles |
| Slug | The slug line | Kept to about 60 characters, 65 at most, so the address is not cut off |
| Excerpt | The excerpt line | Under 300 characters, so it fits cards and search results |
| Body (markdown) | The paragraphs under Body | Plain paragraphs; the preview pane should show them without any symbols |
| Status | Draft (hidden) | Publish after you have checked the posts marked Confirm |
| Publish at | Leave empty | Empty publishes the moment you press publish |
| Where it appears | In the news section on; ticker off | Switch on In the gallery, as a moment for award posts only |
| Category | The category line | Publication for papers, Chatbot for the three chatbot papers, Award for awards |
| Tags | The tags line | Comma separated, never more than 8 |
| External link | The link line | Only where the CV or the lab page gives one; otherwise leave empty |
| Event date | The event date line | A full date when the day is known; the first of the month when only the month is known; empty when only the year is known |

## What the bodies do and do not say

None of them states a result or a number the CV does not give, because the CV lists titles and venues only. If you want findings in a post, paste the abstract's own sentence after the second paragraph.

The People line under each post is the author list as the CV writes it. Initials stay as they are until each person agrees to a full name.
